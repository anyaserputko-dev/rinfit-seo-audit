What we checked

We went through all 227 pages of rinfit.com the way Google and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI answers) see them: how fast pages open, how clearly they explain what you sell, and what stops them from showing your store to shoppers.

Good news: the store is well built. Two things hold it back. The site is very slow on phones: the main picture takes 29.9 seconds to appear. And pages are missing the small texts Google reads: clear names for categories, page descriptions, picture descriptions and links to your blog.

Below are 28 fixes in three groups: do first, next, and nice to have. Each row says what is wrong, what we will do and what you get. Total 67.5 hours, $2,025. The grey Technical details lines are for your developer, you can skip them.

Words your developer may use
  • SEO · everything that helps Google show your pages higher in search.
  • Alt text · a short text description of a picture. Google, AI and screen readers for blind people read it instead of “seeing” the picture.
  • Meta title / description · the blue headline and the two grey lines Google shows for your page in search results.
  • H1, H2, H3 (headings) · the page’s table of contents: H1 is the main title, H2 are chapters, H3 are sub-chapters.
  • Structured data (schema, JSON-LD) · hidden labels in the code that tell Google “this is a product, this is its price, this is the rating”.
  • Lighthouse · LCP · Google’s free site checker. LCP is how long until the biggest thing on the screen (usually the main picture) appears.
  • Noindex · 301 redirect · “hide this page from Google” · “this page has moved here for good”.
  • Open Graph · the picture and text that appear when someone shares your link in a messenger or social media.
