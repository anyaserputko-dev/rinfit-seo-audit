01

CriticalMake the hero image load first on mobile

What to do

Mark the first hero slide loading="eager" and fetchpriority="high", add a <link rel="preload"> for it, and exclude it from the theme lazy-load loop.

Why it matters

Google scores your site on the phone version first. The main image on the phone appears after 29.9 seconds because the theme tells the browser to load it last. Until this is fixed every other speed fix is capped.

What it affectsMobile speedGoogle rankingsConversion
Budget$602 h · $30/h

Critical · task 1 of 8

Technical details

Lighthouse mobile: performance 35, LCP 29.9 s (target under 2.5 s), Speed Index 36.1 s, Time to Interactive 45.7 s. Desktop: 82, LCP 1.8 s.

Raw homepage HTML: 371 images with loading="lazy", 0 with loading="eager", 0 <link rel="preload" as="image">, 2 fetchpriority="high". The LCP element is the first slideshow banner.

Server response is 10 ms and Shopify CDN already serves AVIF (hero returns image/avif, 12 KB), so the delay is entirely on the theme side. Fix lives in the Prestige slideshow section (sections/slideshow.liquid and the image snippet).

02

CriticalRemove duplicate Google tags

What to do

Keep one copy of GA4 and one of Google Ads. Remove the two extra injections of each (theme, app embed or GTM double-firing) and the duplicate reCAPTCHA loader.

Why it matters

The same tracking scripts are downloaded three times, about 700 KB of pure repetition, and every copy runs on the main thread. Double counting can also inflate your GA4 and Ads numbers.

What it affectsMobile speedAnalytics accuracy
Budget$602 h · $30/h

Critical · task 2 of 8

Technical details

gtag/js?id=G-TWY8KMWR3Q is requested 3 times (190 KB each) and AW-826433674 3 times (160 KB each). Total blocking time on mobile is 5,630 ms, JavaScript bootup 24.0 s, main-thread work 36.5 s.

Analytics beacons also fire in loops: Shopify monorail 19 requests, Microsoft Clarity 8 requests per page view. Check layout/theme.liquid, the Google & YouTube channel app embed and any custom pixel for the duplicate sources.

03

CriticalTame the Privy popup script

What to do

Load Privy only after the first user interaction (or after the page is fully painted), or replace it with a lighter signup tool. Review which Privy campaigns are actually running.

Why it matters

One app is responsible for 27 requests and 2.1 MB on every page view, more than all your product images together. On a phone this is several seconds of waiting before the page responds.

What it affectsMobile speedConversion
Budget$1204 h · $30/h

Critical · task 3 of 8

Technical details

Third-party JavaScript is 4,814 KB in 164 requests, 52% of the 9,274 KB mobile page. Privy alone: 27 requests, 2,111 KB (shopify.privy.com). Total page weight target is under 2,000 KB.

Options: defer the Privy script with a user-interaction trigger, restrict it to the homepage and collections, or move signup to the theme newsletter section plus Shopify Email. Measure before and after with Lighthouse mobile.

08

CriticalComplete product structured data for Google Shopping and AI shopping

What to do

Extend the Product JSON-LD with GTIN or MPN, shipping details, the return policy, price validity and per-variant data (ProductGroup with size and colour offers).

Why it matters

AI assistants and Google Shopping read prices, stock, shipping and returns from the code, not from the page text. Today the code has name, price and rating but no shipping, returns, GTIN or variants, so assistants cannot show "free US shipping" or the right size and colour, and merchant listings stay incomplete.
